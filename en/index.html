Direct answer

Can a foreigner register a company in China?

Yes, foreign individuals and overseas entities can establish or invest in companies in China. The available structure and process depend on the proposed activity, foreign-investment market-access rules, location, licences and the investor documents.

The first decision is not the company name. It is the operating model.

Before filing, define what the China company will sell, who it will invoice, where staff and premises will be located, how capital will enter China and whether the activity is restricted or licensed. Registration is one part of market entry—not the end of compliance.
