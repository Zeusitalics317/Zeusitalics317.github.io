STAGE-BY-STAGE TIMELINE

Plan by stage—not by one universal number.

Timing starts only after the activity, city, investor documents, authentication, address and approval requirements are known. Banking, licences and immigration run on separate decisions.

OverviewRequirementsTimelineFAQ

The practical sequence

01

Access and operating-model check

Confirm activity, ownership, city, transactions, licences and foreign-investment restrictions.

02

Name, scope, address and capital plan

Define the registration facts and identify any local premises or pre-approval requirements.

03

Investor documents and authentication

Collect identity or corporate documents, translations, appointments and Apostille or other authentication where required.

04

Company filing and business licence

Submit the agreed company-registration file. The registration authority controls review and acceptance.

05

Post-licence activation

Coordinate official seals, tax onboarding, reporting setup and the selected corporate bank application.

06

Separate optional approvals

Industry licences, import/export, work permits, residence permits and specialist registrations follow their own eligibility and authority timelines.

What commonly changes the schedule

  • Restricted or licensed activity
  • Document errors or authentication route
  • Address and local policy requirements
  • Complex corporate shareholders or beneficial ownership
  • Bank due diligence and expected transactions
  • Authority requests, holidays and resubmissions

After registration

  • Maintain accounting records and required tax filings
  • Submit the enterprise annual report and other required disclosures
  • Keep licences, address and registered information current
  • Record shareholder, director, capital and business-scope changes
  • Handle payroll, social insurance and employment compliance where applicable
  • Arrange an audit when law, industry, investor agreement or another requirement calls for one

We issue a project-specific staged estimate after review. A business licence does not guarantee a bank account, licence, visa, work permit or residence permit.
